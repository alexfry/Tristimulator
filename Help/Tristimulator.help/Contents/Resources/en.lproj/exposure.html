Adjust exposure and view channels

Exposure and channel changes affect only how the image looks on screen.

Adjust exposure

  • Drag the exposure slider in the toolbar. It ranges from −10 to +10 stops.

  • Choose Image > Increase Exposure (or press Command-Right Bracket) or Image > Decrease Exposure (or press Command-Left Bracket) to change exposure by half a stop.

To return to 0, click the reset button in the toolbar, or choose Image > Reset Exposure (or press Command-Backslash).

View a single channel

Choose a channel from the Image menu, or press its key:

ChannelKey
Red ChannelR
Green ChannelG
Blue ChannelB
Alpha ChannelA
Luminance (equal-weight)Y

The toolbar shows which channel is active. To see the full image again, press the same key again.
