Zoom and pan an image

An image opens fitted to its window. Zoom in to inspect detail, then pan to move around.

Zoom in or out

  • Choose View > Zoom In (or press Command-Plus) or View > Zoom Out (or press Command-Minus). Each step changes the magnification by a factor of 1.4.

  • Pinch open or closed on a trackpad.

Zooming leaves fit mode. You can zoom from 10% to 3200%.

See the image at actual size

Choose View > Actual Size (or press Command-0) to show the image at 100%.

Fit the image to the window

Choose View > Zoom to Fit (or press Command-9), or press F.

Pan a zoomed image

Scroll, or drag the scroll bars.
