Keyboard shortcuts in Tristimulator

Many commands in Tristimulator menus have keyboard shortcuts. The colour pipeline doesn’t have one; click the OpenColorIO button in the toolbar.

Symbols: ⌘ Command, ⌥ Option, ⇧ Shift, ← Left Arrow, → Right Arrow.
