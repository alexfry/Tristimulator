Play movies and image sequences

Movies and image sequences show playback controls over the image. The controls hide after about 3 seconds without pointer movement. Move the pointer to show them again.

Play and step through frames

  • Play or pause: Click the Play button, or choose Playback > Play / Pause (or press Space bar).

  • Step one frame: Click the step buttons, or choose Playback > Step Back One Frame or Playback > Step Forward One Frame (or press Left Arrow or Right Arrow).

  • Scrub: Drag along the timeline.

  • Loop: Click the Loop button, or choose Playback > Loop (or press Command-L).

Movies

Movies show timecode and a volume slider.

Image sequences

Sequences have no audio. They show the frame number and a frame rate menu with 12, 23.976, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 48, and 60 fps.

Tip: To move the playback controls, drag an empty part of them.
