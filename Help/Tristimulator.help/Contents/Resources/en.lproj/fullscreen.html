View an image in full screen

Choose View > Enter Full Screen (or press Command-F).

In full screen, the background is black. The toolbar, info bar, and pointer hide after about 2 seconds. Move the pointer to show them again.

The OpenColorIO button is on the floating toolbar, so you can change colour settings without leaving full screen.
