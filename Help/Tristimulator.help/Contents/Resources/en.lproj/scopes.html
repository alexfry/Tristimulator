Show chromaticity diagrams

Chromaticity diagrams show where the image’s colours fall. They appear over the image.

Show or hide a diagram

Choose one of the following from the View menu. Choose it again, or click the diagram’s close button, to hide it.

CommandShortcut
Toggle CIE 1931 Yxy⌘1
Toggle CIE 1976 u′v′⌘2
Toggle JMh⌘3

Move or resize a diagram

Drag the bar at the top of the diagram to move it. Drag its corner to resize it.
